# Author Instructions

## Submission

<div style="text-align: justify">
<p>All submissions must use the official MIDL 2027 <a href=https://github.com/MIDL-Conference/MIDLLatexTemplate> LaTeX template</a> and be submitted through <a href=https://openreview.net/group?id=MIDL.io/2027> OpenReview</a>. No alterations may be made to the font size (main text and tables), line spacing, or margins.</p>
</div>

MIDL 2027 offers two full-paper tracks and one short-paper track:

- **Main track:** up to 10 pages (up to 12 pages after rebuttal) excluding references, acknowledgements, and appendices
- **Special track - Clinical Translation & Validation:** up to 14 pages (up to 16 pages after rebuttal) excluding references, acknowledgements, and appendices
- **Short-paper track:** strictly limited to 3 pages (excluding references); no appendices allowed
- Note: There are no page limits for the references and appendices for the full-paper tracks. However, reviewers are not required to read the appendices to evaluate a submission.

<div style="text-align: justify">
<p>We encourage authors to share code, models, and datasets to promote open science and reproducibility, although this is not required for acceptance. Please refer to <a href=https://www.midl.io/reproducibility> MIDL reproducibility guidelines </a>.</p>

<p>To submit a paper, all authors must have a valid and updated OpenReview account. Please notice that new accounts created without an institutional email will go through a moderation process than can take some time.</p>

<p>Please ensure that your paper is registered on the system before the abstract registration deadline. No new submissions will be accepted after this date.</p>

<p>Full-paper submissions substantially similar to work accepted by, or simultaneously submitted to, another archival conference are not permitted. Posting a preprint or presenting work at a non-archival workshop does not by itself constitute prior or dual publication.</p>
</div>

## Review and Publication
<div style="text-align: justify">
<p>Full-papers will undergo peer review, author rebuttal, and discussion. Reviews will be single-blind: reviewers will know the authors' identities, while reviewer identities will not be disclosed to authors.</p>

<p>Accepted full papers will be published in the <a href=https://proceedings.mlr.press>Proceedings of Machine Learning Research (PMLR)</a>. Selected papers will be invited for oral presentation. Short papers will undergo a lighter review process and will not be included in the PMLR proceedings.</p>

<p>At least one co-author of each accepted paper must register and present the work in person, subject to the conference's accessibility and exceptional-circumstances policies.</p>
</div>

## Authorship Policy

<div style="text-align: justify">
<p>The author list must be final at the time of submission of the full-paper. Additions, removals, or order changes after the submission deadline are not permitted (except minor spelling or affiliation corrections approved by the Program Chairs).</p>
</div>

## Policy on the use of Large Language Models

<div style="text-align: justify">
<p>MIDL 2027 welcomes the responsible use of modern tools, including Large Language Models (LLMs), when preparing submissions, provided that authors maintain scientific integrity, originality, and full responsibility for all content.</p>
</div>

**Permitted uses:** LLMs and other assistive tools may be used only for minor language edits, such as grammar, spelling, style refinement, readability improvements, or formatting.

**Prohibited uses:** Generating research ideas, study designs, analyses, results, or substantive text with an LLM is not allowed. All scientific contributions must originate from the authors.

**Responsibility:** Authors are accountable for the accuracy, originality, and ethical standards of their manuscript, regardless of which tools they used. LLMs may not be credited as authors.

**Data & privacy:** Some tools store or reuse text for model training. Authors should avoid entering sensitive or unpublished data into third-party services unless appropriate safeguards are in place.

**General Guidance:** LLMs must never replace authors’ critical thinking, due diligence, or validation of references and claims; Only humans may be listed as authors; Violations may result in desk rejection, withdrawal after acceptance, or other corrective actions.
